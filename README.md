# Tenz Notifications

Your [Tenz](https://tenz.app) Trackers alerts on the desktop, over other apps, with their Long and Short buttons.

**[Download for Windows](https://github.com/tenz-app/tenz-notifications/releases/latest/download/Tenz-Notifications-Setup.exe)** (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

1. Install it; it runs in the tray. The installer isn't signed yet: if Windows says it protected your PC, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
2. In Tenz, open **Settings › Notifications › Desktop App** and press **Connect**. If your browser asks to let Tenz use apps on this device, allow it.
3. Keep Trackers open in your browser while the app runs: Trackers decides what alerts you and places your trades, and the app shows them.

This repository only hosts the app's releases. A release's files can't change once it's published.
